# Olive & Ember — Project Blueprint

## 1. Brand Identity
- Brand: Olive & Ember
- Tagline: A little warmth, a lot of flavour.
- Business concept: Premium neighbourhood Mediterranean restaurant.
- Personality: Warm, refined, authentic and welcoming.
- Primary goal: Generate reservation enquiries.
- Audience: People seeking a premium but welcoming dining experience.

## 2. Visual Design System

### Colour palette
- Warm ivory: #F5EFE3
- Deep olive: #252B1B
- Terracotta: #B5532E
- Sandstone: #D8C5A8
- Muted text: #5E5A4D

### Typography
- Bodoni Moda: Hero headline and major headings.
- Cormorant Garamond: Brand wordmark and selected editorial details.
- DM Sans: Navigation, body copy, buttons and forms.

### Design principles
- Editorial, spacious and sophisticated.
- Strong contrast between typography and photography.
- Consistent spacing and rounded pill-shaped buttons.
- Avoid clutter, excessive animation and unnecessary decoration.
- Maintain readable text and accessible colour contrast.

## 3. Website Sections

1. Header and navigation
   - Brand wordmark
   - Home, Our Story, Menu, Visit Us
   - Reserve a Table CTA

2. Hero
   - Eyebrow: Mediterranean Kitchen
   - Headline: A little warmth, a lot of flavour.
   - Short supporting copy
   - Reserve a Table and Explore Our Menu buttons
   - Large, warm Mediterranean food photograph

3. Signature dishes
   - Three visually consistent dish cards
   - Dish names, concise descriptions and images
   - Prices only when confirmed

4. Our story and atmosphere
   - Restaurant story and dining philosophy
   - Supporting restaurant photography

5. Guest reviews
   - Use genuine, authorised reviews only.
   - Do not invent testimonials, ratings or customer claims.

6. Visit us and reservations
   - Confirmed location and opening hours
   - Contact details
   - Reservation enquiry form

7. Footer
   - Brand identity
   - Useful navigation
   - Confirmed contact information
   - Relevant social links, if available

## 4. User Journey
Discover the restaurant → Explore dishes → Build confidence →
Find visit information → Submit a reservation enquiry.

Visitors must also be able to access the reservation CTA immediately
from the header or hero.

## 5. Responsive Requirements
- Desktop: Two-column hero, horizontal navigation and three dish columns.
- Tablet: Adjust spacing, typography and image proportions without
  awkward stacking or cramped navigation.
- Mobile: Stacked hero, compact navigation, readable text and
  appropriately sized buttons.
- Prevent horizontal overflow at all screen sizes.
- Ensure images crop appropriately on different devices.

## 6. Technical Requirements
- Semantic HTML5 and accessible page structure.
- CSS custom properties for design tokens.
- Vanilla JavaScript for required interactions.
- Responsive CSS using media queries.
- Accessible navigation, visible focus states and form labels.
- Form validation and a clear success/error response.
- Descriptive page title, meta description and image alt text.
- Optimised images and sensible loading behaviour.
- No unnecessary libraries or paid services.

## 7. Assets and Content
- Store project images and other local assets in the assets folder.
- Use appropriately licensed photography and record its source.
- Use confirmed business details for any real-world claims.
- Keep the restaurant concept clearly fictional in the portfolio
  unless a real business authorises its details and content.

## 8. Implementation Order
1. Project structure and design tokens.
2. Fonts, header and navigation.
3. Desktop hero section.
4. Signature dishes.
5. Story and atmosphere.
6. Reviews and visit/reservation sections.
7. Footer.
8. Tablet and mobile refinements.
9. JavaScript interactions and form integration.
10. Accessibility, SEO, performance and cross-browser checks.
11. GitHub updates, deployment and portfolio presentation.
